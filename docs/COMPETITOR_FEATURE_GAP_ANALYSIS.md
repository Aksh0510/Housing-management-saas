# Competitor Feature Gap Analysis

## Reviewed public product benchmarks
- Dwarjo
- ADDA
- MyGate offerings
- MyGate community management
- NoBrokerHood

## Observed industry patterns
- strong resident self-service experiences
- connected billing, visitor, and complaint flows
- emphasis on onboarding and operational visibility
- mobile-first gate and daily-help use cases

## Where the current project aligns
- role-based permissions and modules are already well-structured
- key demo workflows are connected in a front-end mock layer
- the product already aims to present a polished society operations dashboard

## Where the project still needs work
- deeper multi-step onboarding and migration readiness
- stronger product-level billing and subscription separation
- more explicit operational exception queues and audit outcomes
- more premium UX detail in onboarding and action-heavy screens

## Positioning guidance

The product should differentiate itself with guided onboarding, clear fee-to-receipt traceability, exception-driven management, and fast mobile gate and staff work, without copying competitor branding or flows.

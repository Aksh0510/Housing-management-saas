# Client Demo Script

1. Open the landing page and explain the platform positioning.
2. Click `Explore the demo` and sign in as `Society Admin`.
3. Show the onboarding module, summary cards, and recent approvals.
4. Open a maintenance bill and explain its ledger, status, and payment path.
5. Switch to a resident account and show the corresponding billing visibility.
6. Complete a demo payment and confirm the receipt state.
7. Switch to the accountant view and verify the updated financial state.
8. Submit a complaint as a resident, assign it as a manager, and resolve it as staff.
9. Pre-approve a visitor as resident and check them in/out as the gate team.
10. Review notices, documents, and committee workflows before ending the demo. 

All actions in this script use fictional demo data and should be labelled as such.
